Design brief meets edit timeline

Work starts with a claim the client wants to make and ends with the three or four assets needed to land it. Because the design and the edit come from one desk, the static and the reel share their type, pacing, and colour instead of looking like two suppliers who never spoke.

Recent output has run almost entirely in Hindi and English for Indian audiences. That shapes the typography more than people expect: Devanagari and Latin need different weight and spacing to read equally loud at thumb speed.
